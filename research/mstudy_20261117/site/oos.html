Out-of-sample days

The trained fair models, frozen, re-scored on every other day on the tape, including today's collection.

What held up

  • The ranking holds on unseen days. Pooled over 20 BTC 15-minute days the models never saw, 0.1s R² is: GRU 60 min 0.262, Transformer 75+90 min 0.247, GRU 30 min 0.247, LightGBM 0.235. This almost exactly matches the original single test day.
  • The raw-event models usually win, not always. On BTC 15m, the 60-minute GRU beats LightGBM at 0.1s on 17 of 20 unseen days, and the continued Transformer on 14. The continued Transformer improved on its 75-minute checkpoint on most days, so the extra training generalised.
  • The perp result is the most robust. The perp GRU beats LightGBM on 19 of 19 unseen days, with 0.1s R² of 0.173 against 0.092. LightGBM's perp R² decays toward October (0.03–0.05 on Oct 4–6), while the GRU stays at 0.13–0.24.
  • No decay over 18 days, but today is harder. September and early October scores do not trend down with distance from training. On today's tape (Nov 17, 57 days later, 16 hours, 44 BTC 15m markets), every model drops to about 0.12–0.14 at 0.1s, LightGBM included. Mid moves today are larger and less often zero (std at 0.1s is 1.03 ticks, against 0.81–0.91 in September/October; P(no move) is 0.71, against 0.75–0.76). That points to a shift in the market, not a model-specific failure. The raw-event models still edge out LightGBM (GRU 0.140, LightGBM 0.122).
  • Perps today: GRU 0.142, LightGBM 0.078, Transformer 0.073 at 0.1s, in line with the October range.
  • Caveat: per-day R² swings by several points with the sample drawn. Trust the pooled numbers and day counts more than any single day.

Method

  • The models are the frozen checkpoints from the training runs, with no fine-tuning. Normalisation statistics are recomputed exactly as in training (same files, same seed, train days Sep 18–21).
  • LightGBM is refit with the identical recipe and early-stopped on Sep 22, so its numbers match the original baseline.
  • New days: Sep 15–17, Sep 24 – Oct 9, and Nov 17 (today, collected by kalshi-collector since 08:30 UTC). Each was extracted from the tape and tokenised with the same pipeline (extract.py → features.py … fair). The perp tape starts Sep 16, and Sep 15 BTC 15m is a partial day (5,000 samples).
  • Sampling per day: up to 500 anchors per BTC 15m market, and 15,000 perp anchors. Pooled scores are sample-weighted means of per-day R² over unseen days only. Sep 22 and Sep 23 are shown for reference but excluded from pooling.
  • Code: code/eval_fair.py, code/run_oos.sh, code/oos_summary.py. wandb run fair-out-of-sample (group fair-oos). VM time was about 55 minutes (≈ $1.70).
