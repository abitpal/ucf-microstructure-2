kalshi-analysis is on-demand D32ads_v7 (~$1.82/h), which was already running before this session. kalshi-collector adds roughly $0.13/h. Nothing else was created.

Assumptions to check

  • Kalshi taker fee 0.07·P·(1−P) per contract per side for all binaries (some series have different multipliers).
  • Perp taker fee assumed 2 bp per side.
  • Taker orders = fills with the same exchange millisecond and side; book state taken before the order's own deltas.
  • Sports sampled at most 40 windows and 300 orders per market-day; crypto hourly/range strikes capped similarly, so BTC 15m and perps are not drowned out.
