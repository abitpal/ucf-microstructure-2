Overview

Microstructure Night Run

Does the order book look different from market to market, can the next taker order be judged as toxic or benign, and do raw-event sequence models beat handcrafted features at predicting the fair price?

Headline answers

  • Is crypto structure different from sports? Yes, decisively. With features that ignore volume, price level and tick size, a classifier tells crypto binaries, sports and perps apart with 0.91 balanced accuracy from 16 book events and 0.99 from 1,024. BTC 15m vs sports is ~100%.
  • Does structure differ market to market? Yes. 13 market types are told apart with 0.86 balanced accuracy (chance 0.08). Crypto products and perps are each nearly perfectly separable; sports leagues are distinct but more similar to each other (0.78 within sports).
  • What differs: crypto and perps trade in millisecond bursts and their book imbalance predicts the next second; sports books update more irregularly, imbalance carries almost no information, and taker flow is much less toxic (12–17% of orders see the price run past the fill within 1 s, vs 26% in BTC 15m and ~47% in perps).
  • Can the next taker order be judged? Yes for toxicity: AUC 0.93 (0.1 s) to 0.79 (10 s) on unseen markets, and 0.77–0.92 within each family at 1 s. Large orders that clear the touch after a move in their direction are the toxic ones. The autoencoder and classifier embeddings add nothing beyond the order and window features. Even the model's best 1% of predicted-profitable taker trades still lose money after two fees.
  • Bitter lesson: raw events win, and more compute keeps helping. A GRU over the last 128 raw book events (each event's age encoded in continuous time) beats handcrafted-feature LightGBM at every horizon on a held-out day. BTC 15m: R² 0.262 / 0.164 / 0.123 / 0.018 (60-min run) vs 0.232 / 0.152 / 0.114 / 0.015. BTC perp: 0.187 / 0.078 / 0.046 / 0.005 vs 0.128 / 0.044 / 0.023 / −0.002. The Transformer ties or slightly beats LightGBM; on CPU it gets about a third of the GRU's training steps.

Suggested next steps

  • Use the toxicity model on the maker side: widen or skew quotes when the next order looks like a touch-clearing chaser. That is where this information pays; taking does not clear two fees.
  • Scale the raw-event models on a GPU. Both were still improving when their CPU time ran out; the GRU scaling run will show the slope.
  • Test the models on tonight's fresh tape (Nov 17) for a true out-of-period check.
  • Confirm the Kalshi perp taker fee: 2 bp/side was assumed and drives every perp net-PnL number.

How to read these pages

Every number comes from three Kalshi market families recorded by the existing tape: crypto binaries (BTC 15-minute, alt 15-minute, hourly and range strikes), sports binaries (NFL, NCAAF, MLB, soccer, WNBA, tennis) and perpetual futures (BTC, ETH and alt perps). The colours are used the same way in every figure. Tests are always on markets the models never saw in training.
